How it plays

Five chances. Fewer is better.

Get it right

Party

Same clip, same moment.

Host a room and share the code. Up to 50 friends hear the clip together; the fastest right answer scores the most. Podium at the end.

  • Free to join, on any phone
  • 5, 10 or 15 rounds, your difficulty and era
  • Live scoreboard every round

Ranked

A rating that moves.

Five rounds against an opponent, climbing from Easy to Impossible. Rounds are worth their difficulty, a first-second answer earns a bonus, a wrong guess costs you.

  • Weekly seasons — everyone restarts on Monday
  • Daily and weekly leaderboards
  • Your best matches count, not your most

Questions

Things people ask.

Do I need Apple Music or Spotify?

No. Songspot plays the public 30-second previews from Apple's catalogue. No subscription, no music account.

Can my friends play without buying anything?

Yes. Joining a party is free for everyone, and the web version is free while it's in beta.

Where's the Android version?

Built and in testing. Google Play needs a closed test period before it can go public — it's coming.

What does the game keep about me?

A display name, an optional picture and your stats. Delete it all with one tap in Profile. Full details in the privacy policy.
