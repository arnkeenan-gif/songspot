Questions

Things people ask.

Do I need Apple Music or Spotify?

No. Songspot plays the public 30-second previews from Apple's catalogue. No subscription, no music account.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. It's a one-time purchase on the App Store. Restore it any time with the same Apple ID.

Can my friends play without buying anything?

Yes. Joining a party is free for everyone; only the host needs Premium.

Where's the Android version?

Built and in testing. Google Play needs a closed test period before it can go public — it's coming.

What does the game keep about me?

A display name, an optional picture and your stats. Delete it all with one tap in Profile. Full details in the privacy policy.
